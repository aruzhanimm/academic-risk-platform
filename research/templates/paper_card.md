# Paper evidence card

Use one card per distinct publication. Replace prompts with checked information; retain an explicit unknown where evidence is unavailable.

- **ID and full reference:** authors, year, title, venue, DOI or official URL.
- **Publication type:** peer-reviewed article, conference paper, preprint, technical document, or other.
- **Access/check date:** actual date and the sections or pages read.
- **Research question and population:** setting, recruitment, inclusion criteria, sample size.
- **Data and unit of analysis:** participant, registration, observation, or another unit; distinguish rows from unique people.
- **Outcome:** instrument, construct, scoring, timing, and whether it is concurrent or future.
- **Method:** model or analysis, preprocessing, feature selection, and tuning.
- **Evaluation:** split unit, time ordering, independent test data, baselines, metrics, and uncertainty; mark non-applicable fields explicitly.
- **Results reported by the authors:** preserve numerical context and locate each key claim in a section, table, figure, or page.
- **Limitations:** sampling, measurement, missingness, dependence, leakage, reporting ambiguity, and transfer to the project population.
- **Project implication:** what the source supports, what it does not establish, and any resulting proposed decision.
- **Access/reuse:** data availability, permitted use, questionnaire rights, and unresolved permissions.
- **Review record:** actual reviewer, date, substantive correction or decision; leave blank until reviewed.
