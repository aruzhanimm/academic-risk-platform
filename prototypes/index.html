WHEN AN ESTIMATE BECOMES AVAILABLE

A useful explanation should tell you…

What it describes

The outcome, whether it is a current assessment or a forecast, and the relevant time window.

What informed it

The input observation and, where supported, how the model used its information.

Where its limits are

Uncertainty and applicable limitations. Model explanations do not establish causes or diagnoses.
