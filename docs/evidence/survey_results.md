# Student Questionnaire Results

## Source and counting rules

The questionnaire, [How do you plan your academic workload?](https://forms.gle/LZZL73VuXMRriJzs7), was published in Russian on 23 October 2026. The Forms summary was checked on 24 October 2026 and contained nine submissions, all with the participation acknowledgement. This is a snapshot; subsequent submissions are not included in these counts.

The team distributed the questionnaire through existing contacts. It did not collect email addresses or identifying fields. Nine is therefore the number of submissions, not a verified count of distinct people. No attempt was made to link responses to interview participants. The substantive questions were optional.

### Difficulty understanding upcoming tasks and deadlines

The question refers to the preceding two study weeks. Each submission selected one option.

| Option | Count |
| --- | ---: |

Four submissions report frequent difficulty, while three report it rarely or never. This is evidence of different planning experiences within the submissions, not a measure of burnout.

## Design interpretation

The results favour examining a brief entry flow, clear workload context and flexible use frequency. Existing tools, especially the learning system, are already important to these submissions. A new application should explain its additional purpose and avoid unnecessary duplication. The small convenience sample and unverified uniqueness limit generalisation. No statistical association, prevalence estimate or model result is derived from this questionnaire.
