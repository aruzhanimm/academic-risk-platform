# Project risks

**Planning snapshot: 23 October 2026, Asia/Almaty. Content: Draft. Team review: Pending.**

The immediate objective is the 24 October submission package: documented research and design, a clearly labelled synthetic prototype, actual review and task evidence, and accessible supporting materials. A complete application or trained model is not a prerequisite for this package.

[ARP-10] is In Progress: this register and the management documents are working-branch drafts. Peer review remains pending under [ARP-13]. The [task register](task_register.md) records the fourteen live issues, ARP-2–ARP-15, and their delivery evidence.

Likelihood and impact are qualitative judgements. **High** likelihood indicates a gap threatening the milestone; **Medium** indicates a credible failure requiring a check. High impact blocks a required submission item, invalidates a research claim, or exposes private data. Owners coordinate the response and retain closure evidence. All risks below are open.
